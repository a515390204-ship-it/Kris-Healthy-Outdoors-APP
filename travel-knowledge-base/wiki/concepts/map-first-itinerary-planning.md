# Map-first itinerary planning

Map-first planning means the product generates a city route overview before exposing detailed place points.

## Rules

- Multi-city overview: city nodes, inter-city transport, total travel rhythm.
- Single-city detail: attractions, food, accommodation, neighborhoods, and daily route segments.
- Use map zoom level and selected city to control point density.
- Avoid showing every note-derived point at once.

## Product Link

This concept directly supports the outdoor travel planner demo and future mini program itinerary generation.
